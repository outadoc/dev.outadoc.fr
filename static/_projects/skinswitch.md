SkinSwitch is the most simple skin manager for Minecraft ever designed.

1.  Enter your minecraft.net login/password in the app (don't worry, they're stored safely and will never be used for anything else than changing your skin);
2.  Add as many skins as you want in the application, by getting them from the username of another player that is already wearing it, or by downloading them from their URL;
3.  The list of all your skins is displayed on the main page; you only have to tap **one** button to instantly wear the Minecraft skin you just chose.

You can integrate SkinSwitch with your own website or application by using its URL scheme: for example, click on this link to [add Notch's skin to the app](skinswitch://?name=The%20Creator&desc=Notch,%20or%20Markus%20Persson,%20created%20Minecraft%20in%202009.&url=https://s3.amazonaws.com/MinecraftSkins/Notch.png).

A [web version of SkinSwitch](http://skinmanager.fr.nf) was also created by [EphysPotato](http://twitter.com/EphysPotato), and is used to browse skins inside the app!
