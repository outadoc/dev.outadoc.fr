Are you tired of being subscribed to people that make series you're not interested in? But you don't want to unsubscribe them, because you like some of their videos nevertheless? YT Videos Hider is for you!

This extension allows you to hide certains videos from your Youtube home page based on their title. Just follow the instructions on the options page, save your filters and you're done.
