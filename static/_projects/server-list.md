Server List for Minecraft is the best way to simply manage and control your favorite Minecraft servers.

Either if you are a player or a server administrator that wants to control the status of his server, Server List will seduce you by its simplicity of usage and its lightness.

Everything is done as in the original Minecraft list, except you can just keep your device with the list displayed while you're already playing, you don't have to open the game just to check if there are free slots on your favorite server...

Just click 'add', enter the information about the server, click 'done', and that's it! Your server is added to the main screen.
