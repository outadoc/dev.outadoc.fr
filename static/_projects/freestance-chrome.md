FreeStance for Google Chrome is the web browser version of FreeStance, designed to remote control your Freebox (French set-top box) right from your Internet-browsing device.

The main features of this remote is that it's simple and easy to use, and fits right next to your address bar so it's neither a pain to find nor too intrusive.

You can also check out FreeStance for [iOS](../freestance/) and [desktop](../freestance-desktop/).
