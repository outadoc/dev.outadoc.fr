This website was created to list all of Jérôme Keinborg's "borgueries", which are puns he used to make periodically on AppLoad, a podcast then produced by the NoWatch.net network.

Nothing serious intended, and you'll definitely need to understand French pretty well to enjoy it; if you do though, you should also check out the podcast on [Frenchspin.fr](http://frenchspin.fr). Still as fun!
