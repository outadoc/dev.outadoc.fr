Splat Companion is a simple yet useful app that will inform you of the current Splatoon stages without having to boot up the game!

That's all, really. But it's really nice!

Stage names are localized in English, French, German, Spanish, Italian, and Japanese.
The data is fetched in real time thanks to splatoon.ink's API.
